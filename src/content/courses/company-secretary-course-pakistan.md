This programme prepares learners for the company-secretarial and corporate
affairs function in Pakistan. It connects the Companies Act framework with the
day-to-day documents a secretary actually produces - agendas, board papers,
minutes, resolutions and statutory registers - and with the filings that keep
an entity compliant.

A closing practical workshop brings the modules together, simulating a
secretarial cycle from incorporation through annual compliance obligations.
