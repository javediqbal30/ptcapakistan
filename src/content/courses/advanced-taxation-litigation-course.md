This advanced programme develops the practical skills required to deal with
federal and provincial tax authorities end to end - from compliance through
assessment, audit and the appellate process. It is built for professionals who
want to move past routine return filing and represent clients competently.

The curriculum walks through income-tax and sales-tax compliance, withholding
obligations, audit and assessment proceedings, drafting of notices and grounds
of appeal, stay applications and written submissions. Every module is anchored
in realistic scenarios drawn from current practice, so learners leave able to
apply the work immediately.
