The CHRP prepares learners to run the modern HR function end to end. Ten
structured modules span the employee lifecycle - from workforce planning and
talent acquisition through performance, compensation, learning and strategic
people management - with Pakistani employment practice woven throughout.

The programme suits both newcomers building an HR career and working
professionals formalising their experience, and it is led by a senior corporate
affairs leader with two decades of industry practice.
