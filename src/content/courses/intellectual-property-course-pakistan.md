The Intellectual Property programme gives participants a comprehensive
understanding of intellectual property rights, the legal protection mechanisms
available and the registration procedures that secure them in Pakistan.

It is suited to law and business professionals who advise clients or protect
their own brands, and to entrepreneurs building an IP strategy. Detailed module
coverage and batch schedules are confirmed by the admissions team.
