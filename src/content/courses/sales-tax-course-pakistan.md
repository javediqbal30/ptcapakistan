This certification builds practical command of Pakistan's sales-tax regime,
covering the complete monthly cycle a practitioner performs. Learners move from
registration and compliant invoicing through return filing, adjustments,
refunds and, importantly, responding to notices and preparing for audit.

The course is deliberately hands-on: every topic is tied to a real filing or
document so that students can operate confidently on FBR IRIS and provincial
portals from day one.
