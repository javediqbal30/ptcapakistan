The **ERPNext Installation, Customization & Implementation** course is a comprehensive,
industry-aligned certification program designed to turn participants into qualified ERP functional
and technical consultants.

ERPNext has rapidly become the preferred enterprise resource planning solution for small, medium,
and expanding corporate enterprises across Pakistan and worldwide due to its zero license fees,
modular architecture, and ease of customization. This course bridges the critical market gap between
generic business theory and actual enterprise software deployment.

Learners progress through every phase of real-world ERP operations: from setting up Linux and Docker
environments, installing Frappe Bench, and configuring multi-company accounts to architecting custom
DocTypes, designing multi-stage approval workflows, automating business rules with client and server
scripts, and generating analytical executive dashboards.

The program culminates in a complete, hands-on implementation capstone project where participants
simulate a real client deployment with requirements mapping, user permissions, data migration, and UAT.
