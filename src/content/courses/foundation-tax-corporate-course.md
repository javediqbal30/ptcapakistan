The Foundation Certification is the entry point for anyone starting a career in
Pakistan's tax and corporate environment. It balances two pillars that every
junior professional needs: a working command of income tax and sales tax, and a
grounding in corporate secretarial practice.

Delivered over 48–50 hours, the programme moves from core concepts to hands-on
filing and drafting, so learners can contribute to compliance work from the
outset.
