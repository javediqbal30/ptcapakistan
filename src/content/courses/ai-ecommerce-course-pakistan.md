The **AI-Powered Local E-Commerce Course** is an end-to-end, hands-on training program designed
to teach participants how to build, launch, and scale a profitable e-commerce business inside
Pakistan using the country's most powerful platforms and modern generative AI tools.

Rather than teaching outdated dropshipping theories, this curriculum focuses entirely on the realities
of the Pakistani local market: sourcing products from local manufacturing and wholesale clusters,
selling on **Daraz PK**, creating a branded **Shopify store**, managing Cash-on-Delivery (COD) cash
flow, and driving high-converting orders through targeted **Facebook and Instagram advertising**.

A distinctive edge of this program is its deep integration of modern AI and visual design.
Students learn to use **Canva**, **ChatGPT**, **Claude**, and **Google Flow** to automate copywriting,
create studio-grade product photoshoots without cameras, and generate viral short-form video ads.

By the end of the 6 months, every participant walks away with a live Daraz store, a fully customized
Shopify website, legal FBR registration, connected delivery logistics, and an active advertising
campaign generating real customer sales.
