Filing an income tax return in Pakistan is now a largely online process through
**FBR IRIS**. This guide walks through the essentials: registering, gathering
your documents, filling the correct return, and confirming that you appear on
the Active Taxpayer List (ATL).

## 1. Register and create your IRIS account

If you do not already have one, begin by registering on FBR IRIS to obtain your
National Tax Number (NTN). Keep your CNIC, mobile number and email handy, as the
registration is tied to them. Once your NTN is issued, you can log in and file
returns.

## 2. Gather the documents you will need

Before you start, collect your supporting records for the tax year. Typical
items include:

- Salary certificate or business income summary
- Bank statements and profit-on-savings details
- Details of any property, investments or capital gains
- Withholding tax certificates where applicable

## 3. Choose and complete the correct return

IRIS provides different return types depending on your income sources. Salaried
individuals, business owners and those with capital gains complete different
sections. Fill in each head of income carefully and reconcile it against your
documenting records - the figures you declare should be defensible if the FBR
raises a query.

## 4. Submit, verify and check the ATL

After submission you receive an acknowledgement. Shortly afterwards, confirm
that your name appears on the Active Taxpayer List. Being on the ATL can lower
withholding tax rates on a range of transactions, so it is worth verifying.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Declaring income that does not match your withholding certificates
- Forgetting a secondary source of income
- Missing the filing deadline and losing ATL status
