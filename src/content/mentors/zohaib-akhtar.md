Zohaib Akhtar is a taxation professional with more than 15 years of diversified
experience across domestic and international tax matters, professional advisory
and practical tax training. He currently serves as **Senior Manager Taxation**
with one of Pakistan's leading business groups.

He has previously worked with **Yusuf Adil Chartered Accountants** and
**Sheikha Mufti & Co.**, where he handled income tax and sales tax compliance,
registration, return filing and appellate work across a broad client base.

At PTCA he leads the taxation and litigation curriculum, translating day-to-day
practice - notices, audits, appeals and representation - into structured,
applied lessons for tax consultants, accountants and advocates.
