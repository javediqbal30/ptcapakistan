Rukhsana Rustam Khan brings more than 20 years of experience across corporate
affairs, regulatory compliance, corporate governance, human resources and
professional training. She currently serves as **Senior Vice President –
Corporate Affairs** with one of Pakistan's largest banks.

She is a Certified Director, Certified Trainer and Advocate, and a
**SECP Registered Intermediary**. Her qualifications include an MPA, LL.B, FCIS
and a Diploma in Taxation from IBA CEE.

At PTCA she anchors the corporate and people-management programmes, guiding
learners through company secretarial practice, SECP compliance and modern HR
management.
